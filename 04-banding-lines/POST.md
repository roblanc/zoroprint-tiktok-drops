# Lines in your DTF prints? Check these 6 causes in order (carousel, 8 slides, 1080 × 1350)

Scheduled 2026-10-26 10:00 Europe/Bucharest. Same images on TikTok (photo mode) and Instagram (carousel).

## Caption

Fine lines across your DTF print? It looks like one problem. It has six causes.

Check in this order: clogged nozzles (70% of banding), bidirectional timing, too few passes, media feed, ink starvation, then the file itself. Print a channel test after any fix to see if it was one color or all of them.
