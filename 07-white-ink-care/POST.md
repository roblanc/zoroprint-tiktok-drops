# Keep DTF white ink alive: daily, weekly, monthly (carousel, 8 slides, 1080 × 1350)

## Caption

Your printer "suddenly stopped printing white"? It skipped the routine.

White DTF ink settles, thickens and dries when it sits. Rock the white daily, print a purge strip first, clean only when you see gaps, and never leave it idle more than 2–3 days. Keep the room at 45–60% humidity: dry air causes most winter clogs.
