Causes 1–2: color conversion

1. Converted twice
Photoshop converts, then the RIP converts again. Keep sRGB. Convert once.
2. Wrong profile
A profile made for another print mode gives blue grays and greenish browns.

Causes 3–4: the ink

3. Ink limits too low
Film looks dry and pale? Raise per-channel limits in 10% steps.
4. A weak or wrong channel
A dead yellow turns black purple. Run the channel test and a nozzle check.

Causes 5–6: white and black

5. White too thin
Dull only on dark shirts? Use 100% white density and 10% highlight white.
6. Black generation too high
Darks flat and grayish? Lower GCR from 60% to 40–50%.

Cause 7: the finish and the screen

A screen glows, film reflects
Neon greens and electric blues are outside what CMYK ink can print.
The second press changes it
Parchment gives a matte finish. A Teflon sheet leaves a slight sheen.
