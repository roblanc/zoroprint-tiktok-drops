# Dull DTF colors? Check these 7 causes in order (carousel, 8 slides, 1080 × 1350)

Scheduled 2026-10-28 10:00 Europe/Bucharest. Same images on TikTok (photo mode) and Instagram (carousel).

## Caption

Colors fine on screen, flat on the shirt? It is the most common DTF complaint.

Check in order: double conversion, the wrong profile, ink limits too low, a weak channel, thin white, too much black generation, then the finish. Start with one swatch pressed on a black and a white shirt. Change one setting at a time.
