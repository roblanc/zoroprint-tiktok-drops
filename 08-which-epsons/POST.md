Scheduled 2026-10-30 10:00 Europe/Bucharest. Same images on TikTok (photo mode) and Instagram (carousel).

## Caption

Converting an Epson to DTF? Check where the white goes and whether your RIP can drive it.

The L805, L1800 and ET-8550 / L8180 are the safe picks. The L8050, L18050, XP-15000 and SC-P700/P900 are experimental: head geometry not yet confirmed on hardware. And buy the printer new or barely used.
