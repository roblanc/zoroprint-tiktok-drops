What makes one easy to convert

Two spare channels
DTF color needs only 4 inks. Photo Epsons have 2 more for white.
Two whites, double the flow
Color and white can print in one pass.
A RIP that sends raw commands
Not the Epson driver. The RIP must drive the head directly.

Supported (beta)

L805 · L800 (A4)
White in the cyan + magenta tanks. The L800 is USB only.

Same layout as the L805, wider carriage, roll film. The most converted.

Same head family as the L1800. White in light cyan + light magenta.

One printer, two names. White in photo black + gray. Validated Sept 2026.

How to choose

Smallest, cheapest to run
An A4 L805. Logos, patches and small gang sheets.
Gang sheets and roll film
An A3+ L1800. Best known, so kits and help are easy to find.
Newer head, already validated
The ET-8550 or L8180.
Buy new or barely used
A worn head that sat with dried dye ink never prints reliable white.
