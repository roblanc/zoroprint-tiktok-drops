≈160 °C

LOWER THE HEAT
140–150 °C
For 10–12 s, light pressure, on polyester
Every 10 °C below 160 halves the migration. Do not add time to compensate: time is exposure.

Powder and film

Low-temperature TPU powder
Melts at about 100–110 °C, so you can press cooler.
Anti-migration film
Adds a thin barrier layer. Worth it on red, black and neon polyester.
Cure fully in the oven
The press should only re-melt the powder, not cure it.

Scorch marks and shine

✕ The problem
  • A shiny rectangle around the design
  • Fibers flattened by heat and pressure
✓ The fix
  • Lower temperature, light pressure
  • Teflon sheet, parchment > platen
  • Pressing pillow under textured weaves
