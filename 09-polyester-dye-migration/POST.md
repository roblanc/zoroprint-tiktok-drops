## Caption

White logo perfect off the press, pink by morning? That is dye migration.

Polyester dyes turn back into gas around 160 °C. Press at 140–150 °C for 10–12 s, use low-temperature powder and anti-migration film, and run white density at 100%. Then test on a sleeve and wait 24 hours before you judge.
