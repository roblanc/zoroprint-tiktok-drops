## Caption

What does one DTF transfer cost you? Start from your numbers, not the shop next door.

A4 example at 70% coverage: color $0.18, white $0.62, film $0.40, powder $0.10 = $1.30, or $0.22 per logo with six nested. Then add 15–25% waste and your time. Nesting is where the margin is.
