## Caption

Why does a DTF print get a white halo on a dark shirt? 👀 A soft glow or feather in your PNG makes half-see-through pixels, and every one of them gets white ink. Export with a hard edge. Full guide: zoroprint.com/guides/white-halo-dtf

## Pinned comment

Quick test: open your PNG over a black background and zoom to 400 % on the edge. If you see a soft fade, the RIP will print white under it. ZoroPrint shows "soft edges → white halo" on the image card, and Fix makes the edge hard.
