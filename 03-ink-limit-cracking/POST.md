## Caption

Your DTF transfer cracks after ten washes? That is too much ink in one spot.

With no limit, one dark pixel can get 400% ink plus a full white base. Cap it with a total ink limit of 250–280%, set black generation (GCR) to 60–80%, and trim per-channel limits if solids puddle. Change one setting at a time.
