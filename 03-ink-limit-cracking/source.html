Fix 2: black generation (GCR)

What it does
Swaps the gray part of a color (equal C, M and Y) for black ink.
Why it helps
Less total ink for the same color, sharper dark detail, a thinner film.
Where to start
60% is a safe default. Use 80% for designs with heavy dark shading.

Fix 3: per-channel limits

Start at C 80 · M 80 · Y 70 · K 70

A solid looks puddled?

Read the symptom

Cracks along dark areas
Total ink limit too high. Drop it to 250%.
Feels like a rubber patch
Ink limit and white both too high. Try 260% and 80% white.
Darks glossy, smear on film
Per-channel limits too high. Lower them by 10%.
Darks flat and grayish
GCR too high. Bring it down to 40–50%.
